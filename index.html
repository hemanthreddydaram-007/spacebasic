📌 How to Get User ID & Authorization Token (Click to toggle)
Step 1
Open SpaceBasic in your browser (Chrome/Edge) and log into your account.
Step 2
Press F12 (or right-click $\rightarrow$ Inspect) and click on the Network tab.
Step 3
Refresh the page or click any menu tab. Select any network request (like profile or student).
Step 4
Look under Headers $\rightarrow$ Request Headers to copy your Authorization token and User-ID.
